# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Everyone participating in Caissa is expected to be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

Feedback should address the code, design, or decision—not the worth or identity of a contributor. Assume good faith, ask clarifying questions, and prefer evidence over escalation.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this standard. Report concerns privately through the contact path in `SECURITY.md` or directly to the repository owner on GitHub.
